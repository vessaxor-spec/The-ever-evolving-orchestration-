# Research Roadmaps

Non-normative strategic research directions for possible future TEO evolution.

Research roadmaps guide investigation. They do not change current policy, release contracts, routing authority, or reference behavior unless a later reviewed change updates those canonical surfaces explicitly.

## Current roadmap research

- [`intelligence-control-plane.md`](intelligence-control-plane.md) explores TEO as a vendor-neutral intelligence control plane above execution runtimes, including outcome learning, runtime neutrality, economic allocation, verification orchestration, benchmarking, and future conformance.
- [`host-integration-contract.md`](host-integration-contract.md) explores a bounded embedding contract for pre-existing AI hosts, covering host-identity preservation, executable TEO version binding, context projection, host-native capability adapters, autonomy and authority semantics, verification capability, recursion budgets, and explicit conformance declarations.
