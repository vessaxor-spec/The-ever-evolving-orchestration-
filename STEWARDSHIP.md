# Stewardship

TEO is maintained as a public, vendor-neutral orchestration project.

## Steward responsibilities

- Preserve the project scope and constitutional principles.
- Require evidence for material routing changes.
- Keep provider-specific claims dated and traceable.
- Separate stable orchestration principles from fast-changing model data.
- Prevent private, proprietary, or identifying information from entering the repository.
- Record significant changes clearly enough for future stewards to understand them.

## Decision standard

A proposed addition should improve orchestration quality, reduce meaningful risk, or make the framework easier to implement. Additions that only increase complexity should be deferred.
