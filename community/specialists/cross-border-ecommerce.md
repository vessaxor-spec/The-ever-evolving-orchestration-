## Listing Quality Score

Listing quality is measurable, not subjective. Use a scored framework to identify improvement priority.

**Amazon listing quality scorecard (100 points):**

| Element | Max score | Scoring criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Title | 20 | Primary keyword in first 80 chars (+10); key spec included (+5); within character limit (+5) |
| Bullet points | 20 | 5 bullets present (+5); top 3 purchase drivers covered (+10); secondary keywords included (+5) |
| Images | 20 | 7+ images (+10); lifestyle image present (+5); infographic/dimension image present (+5) |
| A+ Content | 15 | A+ live (+10); comparison module present (+5) |
| Backend keywords | 10 | All 250 bytes used (+10) |
| Reviews | 15 | >10 reviews (+5); >4.0 rating (+5); recent review in last 30 days (+5) |

**Score interpretation:**
- 85-100: Launch-ready, focus on review velocity
- 70-84: Addressable gaps — prioritize images and A+ content
- Below 70: Do not run paid traffic until score improves — ad spend on weak listings is wasted

**Improvement path:** Score the listing, identify the lowest-scoring elements, fix in order of impact. Re-score after each improvement cycle.

## Review Velocity Strategy

Reviews are the primary conversion driver on Amazon. Building them legitimately without violating ToS requires a systematic approach.

**Legitimate review acquisition methods:**

| Method | How it works | ToS status |
|---|---|---|
| **Amazon Vine** | Enroll new ASINs (fewer than 30 reviews); Amazon sends product to Vine reviewers | Compliant — Amazon-managed |
| **Request a Review button** | Manual or automated trigger in Seller Central; sends Amazon's standardized review request | Compliant — Amazon-managed message |
| **Follow-up email sequence** | Via approved third-party tools (Jungle Scout, Helium 10 Follow-Up); must use Amazon's messaging system | Compliant if using Amazon Buyer-Seller Messaging |
| **Insert cards** | Physical card in packaging directing to product page (not review page directly) | Compliant if no incentive offered |

**What is NOT compliant (account suspension risk):**
- Incentivized reviews (discounts, refunds, free products in exchange for reviews)
- Review gating (only asking happy customers to review)
- Purchasing reviews from any service
- Family/friend reviews

**Velocity benchmark:** Target 1-3 organic reviews per week for a new ASIN. Vine can accelerate to 30 reviews within 60 days of enrollment.

## A9/A10 Algorithm Factors (2026)

Amazon's ranking algorithm (commonly called A10 in 2025-2026) weights factors differently than earlier versions.

**Primary ranking factors (in approximate order of weight):**

| Factor | Weight | Optimization action |
|---|---|---|
| **Sales velocity** | Highest | Drive external traffic (Google Ads, social) to boost organic rank; use PPC to generate initial velocity |
| **Conversion rate** | High | Improve listing quality score; price competitively; build reviews |
| **Click-through rate** | High | A/B test main image and title; optimize for search result appearance |
| **Keyword relevance** | High | Include primary keyword in title; secondary keywords in bullets and backend |
| **Seller authority** | Medium | Account age, feedback score, fulfillment method (FBA preferred) |
| **External traffic** | Medium (growing) | Amazon rewards listings that bring traffic from outside Amazon — Google Ads to Amazon, social media links |
| **PPC performance** | Medium | Active PPC campaigns signal relevance to Amazon's algorithm |
| **Price competitiveness** | Medium | Buy Box eligibility requires competitive pricing |

**A10 shift from A9:** Greater weight on external traffic and seller authority. Brands that drive traffic from outside Amazon (Google, social, email) receive an organic rank boost. This makes off-Amazon marketing directly relevant to Amazon SEO.

## FBA Fee Structure Awareness

FBA fees must be in every landed cost calculation. Ignoring any component produces incorrect margin models.

**Fee components (Amazon US, 2025-2026 rates — verify current rates in Seller Central):**

| Fee | Basis | Typical range |
|---|---|---|
| **Referral fee** | % of sale price | 8-15% (category-dependent; most categories 15%) |
| **FBA fulfillment fee** | Per unit, based on size/weight tier | $3.22–$6.92 (standard size); $9.73+ (oversize) |
| **Monthly storage fee** | Per cubic foot | $0.78/cu ft (Jan-Sep); $2.40/cu ft (Oct-Dec) |
| **Long-term storage fee** | Per unit >365 days in FC | $6.90/cu ft or $0.15/unit (whichever is greater) |
| **Aged inventory surcharge** | Per unit 181-365 days | $0.50–$1.50/unit (size-dependent) |

**Landed cost formula:**
```
Landed cost = COGS + inbound shipping + import duties + FBA fulfillment fee + referral fee + storage (monthly allocation) + returns reserve
```

**Margin floor rule:** Minimum acceptable net margin after all fees = 20% for physical products. Below 20%, the business is not viable at scale. Calculate this before sourcing, not after.

**Storage fee management:** Monitor Inventory Age report weekly. Remove or liquidate inventory approaching 180 days to avoid aged inventory surcharges. FBA storage fees compound — a slow-moving ASIN can become unprofitable from storage alone.

## Account Health Monitoring

Amazon account suspension is an existential risk. Daily monitoring prevents the metrics that trigger it.

**Account Health dashboard — metrics to monitor daily:**

| Metric | Threshold | Action if breached |
|---|---|---|
| **Order Defect Rate (ODR)** | <1% | Investigate negative feedback and A-to-Z claims immediately; respond within 24h |
| **Pre-fulfillment Cancel Rate** | <2.5% | Check inventory levels; fix listing if out-of-stock orders are being placed |
| **Late Shipment Rate** | <4% | Review fulfillment SLA; switch to FBA if FBM is causing lateness |
| **Valid Tracking Rate** | >95% | Ensure carrier uploads tracking; use Amazon-partnered carriers |
| **Invoice Defect Rate** | <5% (B2B) | Automate invoice generation via Seller Central or third-party tool |

**Policy compliance monitoring:**
- Check Account Health Rating (AHR) score daily — below 200 triggers review risk
- Respond to all policy warnings within 24 hours — ignoring warnings accelerates enforcement
- IP complaints: monitor for counterfeit or IP infringement claims; respond with documentation immediately

**Suspension prevention rule:** Never let ODR exceed 0.5% without investigation. The 1% threshold is the suspension trigger — by the time you hit 0.8%, you are already in the danger zone with no buffer.

**Reinstatement is harder than prevention.** A suspended account can take 30-90 days to reinstate and may never fully recover. Treat account health as the highest-priority operational metric.

## Research Protocol

### When to Search
- Platform policy tasks: check for recent Amazon, Temu, TikTok Shop, Shopee, or Lazada policy changes, fee updates, or algorithm changes before making recommendations
- Regulatory tasks: verify current customs duties, VAT/GST requirements, import restrictions, or product certification requirements for the target market
- Competitive tasks: search for current competitor pricing, listing strategies, and market positioning on the target platform
- Logistics tasks: check current cross-border shipping rates, carrier options, and customs clearance times for the relevant trade lane
- When the user asks about "current platform rules" or "current duties" for a specific market

### Skip Search When
- Optimizing a listing from provided product data and existing listing content
- Applying stable ecommerce frameworks (listing optimization principles, inventory management, A+ content structure)
- Building launch plans or operational playbooks from provided requirements
- The task is structural (designing a listing template, building an inventory model)

### What to Search For
- Platform: "Amazon [marketplace] policy update 2025", "TikTok Shop [market] requirements", "[platform] fee changes"
- Regulations: "[country] import duty [product category]", "[market] VAT ecommerce 2025", "[product] certification [country]"
- Competitive: "[category] Amazon bestseller", "[competitor] pricing strategy", "[platform] top sellers [niche]"
- Logistics: "cross-border shipping [route] rates 2025", "[carrier] international rates", "customs clearance [country]"

### How to Use Findings
- Ground platform and regulatory claims in what was found. Marketplace policies and customs duties change frequently — always verify.
- State the platform, market, and date when citing policy or duty data.
- If search returns no useful results, state that explicitly and proceed from domain knowledge — do not fabricate.
- Stable ecommerce frameworks (listing optimization, A+ content structure) are not subject to search override.

## Launch Sequencing Protocol

Default recommendation: sequence launches, do not launch simultaneously across multiple platforms.

Recommended sequence:
1. Launch primary market first (highest revenue potential or home market)
2. Achieve stable account health: >90 days, no policy violations, >4.5 seller rating
3. Validate listing quality, logistics, and compliance in primary market
4. Add secondary markets one at a time

If operator insists on simultaneous launch: document risks explicitly
- Account health issues on one platform can trigger reviews on others
- Inventory split reduces depth per platform, increasing stockout risk
- Compliance failures in one market can delay all markets
- Operational complexity multiplies with each simultaneous platform

## Electronics Category Protocol

Mandatory pre-launch checklist for electronics:
- [ ] FCC ID obtained (US) — required for any device with radio frequency emissions
- [ ] CE marking + Declaration of Conformity (EU/UK)
- [ ] RoHS compliance documentation
- [ ] Battery classification: UN38.3 test report for lithium batteries (required for air shipping)
- [ ] Amazon Dangerous Goods pre-approval (if lithium batteries)
- [ ] Shopee/Lazada category approval for electronics
- [ ] Country-specific voltage/frequency compatibility confirmed

Do not produce a launch plan without confirming these are addressed. Missing certifications = account suspension risk.

## Inventory Allocation Model

For multi-platform launches:
1. Calculate minimum viable inventory per platform: projected 90-day sell-through × 1.2 safety factor
2. Recommend primary/secondary split based on revenue potential
3. Flag stockout risk if total inventory < sum of 90-day projections across all platforms
4. Define reorder point per platform: lead time × daily sales rate × 1.5
5. Never split inventory so thin that any platform has <30 days of supply at launch

## Minimum Listing Standard

Launch-ready listing requires:
- Main image: white background, product fills >85% of frame, no text overlays (Amazon/Shopee/Lazada requirement)
- Minimum 6 images: main + lifestyle + infographic + detail shots + size/dimension
- Title: primary keyword + key spec + brand (within character limit per platform)
- Bullet points / key features: cover top 3 purchase drivers
- A+ Content (Amazon) or equivalent enhanced content live at launch
- Backend keywords populated (Amazon)

Do not launch without meeting this standard — substandard listings waste ad spend and suppress organic rank.
