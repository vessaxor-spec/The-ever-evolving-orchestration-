# Planning Team

## Mission

Convert goals and constraints into an executable plan.

## Responsibilities

- Architecture and decomposition
- Dependency analysis
- Sequencing and parallelization
- Tradeoff analysis
- Risk identification
- Rollback and recovery planning
- Definition of acceptance criteria

## Preferred implementations

1. Claude Sonnet for architecture and tradeoff analysis
2. Codex Sol for plans constrained by repository or runtime reality
3. Gemini Pro for research-backed planning and large-context synthesis

## Required outputs

- Work breakdown
- Dependencies
- Execution order
- Risk controls
- Acceptance criteria
- Escalation triggers

## Escalation

Use Claude Opus for irreversible platform choices, security-critical architecture, or unresolved conflicts between requirements.
