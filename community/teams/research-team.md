# Research Team

## Mission

Collect, compare, and synthesize external or repository-wide evidence required for a decision.

## Responsibilities

- Primary-source research
- Documentation and standards review
- API and dependency research
- Large-context synthesis
- Repository mapping
- Contradiction and uncertainty reporting
- Source traceability

## Preferred implementations

1. Gemini Pro for deep research and large-context synthesis
2. Gemini Flash for fast collection, extraction, mapping, and multimodal triage
3. Claude Sonnet for source challenge and synthesis review
4. Codex Sol when technical claims require repository or executable validation

## Required outputs

- Findings
- Sources
- Conflicts
- Uncertainty
- Implications for the active decision

## Verification

Research that affects consequential decisions must be challenged by a separate reviewer and grounded in primary sources where available.
