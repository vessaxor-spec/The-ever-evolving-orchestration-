# History

Project history and major decision context that no longer represents current operational authority.

Historical records preserve what TEO believed, staged, activated, audited, or validated at a particular point in time. They should not be rewritten to make earlier states look current.

## Lifecycle areas

- [`activation/`](activation/) preserves staged and activated organizational, team, worker, and specialist topology records.
- [`audits/`](audits/) preserves dated reviews, specialist refreshes, routing audits, and recalibration records.
- `validation/` is reserved for completed validation and evidence-result records when such material is migrated or added.

Current normative control remains in active `policy/`, registry, community, and reference surfaces.

Accepted public time capsules remain separately preserved under [`community/capsules/`](../../community/capsules/). Their immutability contract is not changed by documentation-history organization.
