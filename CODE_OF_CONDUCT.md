# Code of Conduct

TEO is a public technical project. Participation must remain respectful, evidence-based, and focused on improving orchestration quality.

## Expected conduct

- Critique ideas, evidence, and implementations, not people.
- State uncertainty and distinguish observation from interpretation.
- Use public information only.
- Do not disclose private prompts, credentials, proprietary workflows, employer information, or identifying operational data.
- Keep discussions relevant to intelligent orchestration.
- Accept correction when stronger evidence is presented.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate misinformation, doxxing, spam, and disclosure of private or confidential information are not permitted.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject content that violates these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in restricted participation.
